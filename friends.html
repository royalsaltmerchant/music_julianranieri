Living Credits

Friends & Affiliates

Music has always reached me through people: teachers, family, bands, studios, friends with half-finished songs, and collaborators.

This is a living thank-you to the people, schools, venues, studios, projects, and communities that shaped my musical life. It is not complete, and it probably never will be. Some names are linked, others are here simply because they mattered.
